Car Sound Kit Control is an independent, unofficial app that opens and closes the exhaust valves on a Car Sound Kit Bluetooth receiver from your Android phone. It is not made, endorsed, or supported by Akrapovič. The Akrapovič name is used only to identify compatible hardware. It is for owners who already have the receiver in the car.

Use the controls only while the vehicle is parked. Do not change the valves while driving.

What you can do
• Scan for the receiver, save it, and reconnect when you return to the car
• Open or close the valves when the receiver reports a known state
• Drive mode applies your preferred position once when you get in; a manual change stays until you leave the car
• Quiet start holds the valves closed for a few minutes, then opens them, with a countdown on the phone
• Two phones in the same car share one receiver: the phone with the car keeps it, and the other waits
• A notification, a Quick Settings tile, and a home-screen widget work while the phone is connected
• On Android Auto, Open and Close work only while parked

The app stays on the phone. It does not use the internet, create an account, or upload logs. A diagnostics file leaves the device only when you choose to share it.

Changing exhaust valves can affect noise, emissions, and warranty. You are responsible for local rules. The app shows this notice on first launch and waits for you to accept it.
