# Privacy policy

**Car Sound Kit Control**  
Last updated: 18 October 2026

Car Sound Kit Control is an independent app published by Apps for Good Product Studio. It is not affiliated with Akrapovič.

## What the app does

The app talks to a Car Sound Kit Bluetooth receiver so you can open or close the exhaust valves while the vehicle is parked.

## Information the app does not collect

The app does not request internet access. It does not create an account. It does not collect, sell, or upload personal information, location, or usage analytics.

## Information that stays on your phone

Settings, the receivers you save, the car Bluetooth address you choose, and connection preferences stay in app storage on the device. They are excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer.

A diagnostics log and any crash text also stay on the device. They leave the phone only if you choose to copy or share them, for example by emailing support@appsforgood.net.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth to find the receiver and send valve commands. On Android 12 and newer it asks for the nearby-devices permission and does not ask for location. On older Android versions, Bluetooth scanning required the location permission that the platform demanded at the time.

## Android Auto

On a car display, the app shows only valve status and Open, Close, and Retry controls. It reads no vehicle data.

## Children

The app is not directed at children.
